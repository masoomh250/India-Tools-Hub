About India Tools Hub

India Tools Hub is a collection of simple browser based calculators and utilities designed for everyday use in India.

Our approach

We aim to keep tools easy to understand, fast to use and transparent about assumptions. Financial and tax calculators are estimates and should not replace professional advice or official information.

Privacy

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser for the tools on this site. If advertising or analytics services are added in the future, this page and the privacy policy will be updated accordingly.
