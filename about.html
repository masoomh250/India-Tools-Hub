About us

Useful tools without unnecessary complexity.

India Tools Hub is a lightweight collection of calculators and utilities for everyday decisions in India. The goal is simple: make common calculations understandable, fast and accessible on mobile and desktop.

What we build

We focus on practical tools for finance, investments, tax, salary, travel, health, dates, shopping and general utility.

Our approach

  • Clear inputs and readable results
  • Simple explanations alongside calculators
  • Browser-based calculations where practical
  • Regular review of time-sensitive financial and tax information

Some tools use assumptions and simplified models. Always verify important financial, tax or health decisions with an appropriate professional or official source.
