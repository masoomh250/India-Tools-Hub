What Is an Age Calculator?

An age calculator determines the elapsed calendar time between a date of birth and a chosen calculation date.

How Is Exact Age Calculated?

Unlike simply dividing the difference in days by 365, an exact calendar calculation accounts for different month lengths and leap years.

Why Do Month Lengths Matter?

Exact age is calculated by comparing the year, month and day components of two dates and borrowing across months when necessary.

Age Calculation Example

For example, if a person was born on 15 January 1993 and the calculation date is 26 November 2026, the result is expressed in complete years, months and days rather than as a rough decimal age.
