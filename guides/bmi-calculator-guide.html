What Is BMI?

BMI stands for Body Mass Index. It is a screening measure based on body weight relative to height.

How Is BMI Calculated?

BMI is calculated using weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared.

Adult BMI Categories

Common adult categories are underweight below 18.5, healthy weight from 18.5 to below 25, overweight from 25 to below 30, and obesity at 30 or above.

BMI Example

For example, a person weighing 70 kg and measuring 1.70 m has a BMI of approximately 24.2.

Limitations of BMI

BMI does not directly measure body fat and can be misleading for some people, including highly muscular individuals. Children and teenagers require age and sex specific interpretation.
