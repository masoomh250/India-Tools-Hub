What Is Date Difference?

A date difference calculator measures the time between two calendar dates.

How Are Dates Compared?

Elapsed days count the actual days between two dates. An inclusive count includes both the starting and ending dates.

Elapsed Days vs Inclusive Days

Months and years are calendar concepts, so their lengths are not constant. A calendar-aware calculation therefore differs from simply dividing total days by 30 or 365.

Date Difference Example

For example, 24 November 2026 to 24 December 2026 is one calendar month and 30 elapsed days. The inclusive count is 31 days.

Why Leap Years Matter

Leap years add an extra day to February and must be handled correctly when calculating exact calendar differences.
