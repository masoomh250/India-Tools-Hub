What Does an Income Tax Calculator Do?

An income tax calculator provides an estimate of tax based on the inputs and tax rules selected by the calculator.

Taxable Income vs Gross Income

Gross income is not necessarily the same as taxable income. Exemptions, deductions, standard deduction and other applicable provisions can affect taxable income depending on the regime and assessment year.

How Slab-Based Tax Works

Under a slab-based system, different portions of taxable income can be taxed at different rates. The final calculation can also include applicable cess and other provisions.

Tax Calculation Example
