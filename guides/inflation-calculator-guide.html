What Is Inflation?

Inflation is a sustained increase in the general price level of goods and services over time.

Inflation Formula

A simplified future-cost estimate uses Future Cost = Present Cost × (1 + inflation rate)^years.

Inflation Example

For ₹1,00,000 with an assumed 6% annual inflation rate for 10 years, the estimated future cost is approximately ₹1,79,084.

Purchasing Power

Purchasing power is the amount of goods and services that a fixed amount of money can buy. Higher inflation generally reduces purchasing power over time.

Why Long-Term Inflation Matters

Actual inflation varies across categories and periods, so a calculator estimate is an assumption rather than a forecast.
