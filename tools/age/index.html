About this tool

Fast and accurate age calculation

Enter your date of birth and the date on which you want to calculate your age. The calculator works directly in your browser.

It calculates your completed years, months and days while also providing useful birthday and date information.

This calculator is intended for general informational purposes.

How to use the Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth and select the date on which you want to calculate your age. By default, the calculator uses today's date. Click Calculate Age to see your exact age.

What does the Age Calculator show?

The calculator provides your exact completed age in years, months and days. It also calculates the approximate total number of days, weeks and months since your date of birth.

How age is calculated

Age is calculated by comparing the date of birth with the selected calculation date. The calculator first determines the completed years, then the remaining months and days after those completed years.

For example, if a person was born on 15 January 1995 and the calculation date is 20 November 2026, the result is 31 years, 10 months and 5 days.

Why use an Age Calculator?

An age calculator can be useful when you need to determine your exact age for applications, forms, eligibility checks, financial planning, school or college requirements, insurance documentation, retirement planning and other situations where your date of birth is required.

Next birthday

The calculator also shows your next birthday and the number of days remaining until it. If your birthday falls on the current date, it will show that today is your birthday.

Day of birth

The calculator identifies the day of the week on which you were born, such as Monday, Tuesday or Sunday.

Zodiac sign

Your zodiac sign is shown based on your birth date using the commonly used Western zodiac date ranges. Zodiac information is provided for general interest and does not represent a scientific assessment.

How accurate is the Age Calculator?

The calculator uses calendar dates to determine your completed years, months and days. It also accounts for different month lengths and leap years.

Can I calculate my age on a past date?

Yes. Enter the past date in the "Calculate age as of" field to determine how old you were on that date.

Can I calculate my age on a future date?

Yes. You can enter a future date to calculate the age you will have reached on that date.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. The calculation uses calendar dates and accounts for leap years when calculating the difference between dates.

What is my age in total days?

The calculator estimates the total elapsed number of days between your date of birth and the selected calculation date.

Can I calculate my next birthday?

Yes. The result shows your next birthday and the number of days remaining until it.

Is this Age Calculator free?

Yes. India Tools Hub calculators are free to use without registration.

Are my date of birth details stored?

The calculation runs in your browser. The static calculator does not require your date of birth to be sent to a server for the calculation.
