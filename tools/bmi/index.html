What is BMI?

Body Mass Index (BMI) is a numerical measure calculated from a person's weight and height. It is commonly used as a screening measure to help classify weight status in adults.

The BMI formula is:

BMI = Weight (kg) ÷ Height (m)²

Adult BMI Categories

For adults aged 20 years and older, the standard BMI categories are:

Underweight: BMI below 18.5

A BMI below 18.5 falls in the underweight category.

Healthy weight: BMI 18.5 to 24.9

A BMI from 18.5 to less than 25 falls in the healthy weight category.

Overweight: BMI 25.0 to 29.9

A BMI from 25 to less than 30 falls in the overweight category.

Obesity: BMI 30 or higher

A BMI of 30 or higher falls in the obesity category. Obesity can be further classified into Class 1, Class 2 and Class 3.

How to use the BMI Calculator

How BMI is calculated

First convert height from centimetres to metres. Then divide your weight in kilograms by the square of your height in metres.

For example, if a person weighs 70 kg and is 170 cm tall:

BMI = 70 ÷ (1.70 × 1.70) = 24.2

This falls within the standard healthy weight BMI range for adults.

Estimated Healthy Weight Range

For adults, the standard healthy BMI range is 18.5 to less than 25. This calculator uses that range to estimate the corresponding weight range for your entered height.

The estimated range is calculated from:

Minimum healthy weight = 18.5 × height²

Maximum healthy weight = 24.9 × height²

Important information about BMI

BMI is useful as a screening measure, but it does not directly measure body fat or distinguish between muscle mass and fat mass. People with different body compositions can have the same BMI.

BMI should therefore be considered along with other factors such as overall health, physical activity, medical history and other health measurements.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a healthy BMI for adults?

For adults aged 20 years and older, a BMI from 18.5 to less than 25 is classified as the healthy weight range under the standard adult BMI categories.

What BMI is considered overweight?

For adults, a BMI from 25 to less than 30 is classified as overweight.

What BMI is considered obesity?

For adults, a BMI of 30 or higher is classified as obesity. It can be further divided into Class 1, Class 2 and Class 3 obesity.

Does BMI apply to children?

BMI is interpreted differently for children and teenagers because their BMI is assessed using age and sex specific growth references. This calculator is designed for standard adult BMI calculations.

Can BMI determine how much body fat I have?

No. BMI does not directly measure body fat. It is a screening measure that should be considered alongside other health information.
