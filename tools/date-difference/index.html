How to use the Date Difference Calculator

Enter the start date and end date, then click Calculate Difference. The calculator automatically identifies which date comes first and calculates the difference between them.

What does the calculator show?

The calculator provides the difference between the selected dates in years, months and days. It also provides the total elapsed days, complete weeks, remaining days and total calendar months.

Calendar difference vs total days

A calendar difference such as 2 years, 3 months and 10 days is different from simply dividing the total number of days by an average month length. Months have different numbers of days, and leap years can add an extra day.

For this reason, the calculator provides both the exact calendar difference and the total elapsed number of days.

How the date difference is calculated

The calculator first determines the earlier and later date. It then calculates the completed calendar years, followed by the remaining months and days. The total day difference is calculated separately using calendar dates.

Inclusive vs exclusive day count

The main result shows the elapsed difference between the two dates. For example, the difference between 1 January and 2 January is 1 day.

The calculator also shows an inclusive count, which counts both the start date and the end date. Therefore, 1 January through 2 January represents 2 calendar days when counted inclusively.

Common uses

Date difference calculations are useful for calculating project durations, notice periods, employment periods, travel duration, subscription periods, deadlines, event planning and the number of days between important dates.

How many days are between two dates?

Enter the two dates into the calculator to get the exact elapsed number of days between them.

Does the calculator account for leap years?

Yes. The calculation uses calendar dates and accounts for leap years when calculating the total number of elapsed days.

Does the calculator account for different month lengths?

Yes. The calendar difference uses the actual month boundaries rather than assuming that every month contains the same number of days.

What is the difference between inclusive and exclusive days?

The elapsed day difference does not count the starting date as a completed day. The inclusive count includes both the start date and the end date.

Can I enter the dates in reverse order?

Yes. The calculator automatically identifies the earlier and later date and calculates the absolute difference.

Can I calculate the number of weeks between two dates?

Yes. The result shows the number of complete weeks and the remaining days.

Can I calculate the difference between dates in different years?

Yes. You can enter dates from different years, including dates spanning multiple years and leap years.
