What is a Discount?

A discount reduces the original selling price of a product or service by a specified percentage or amount.

For example, if a product costs ₹5,000 and has a 20% discount, the discount amount is ₹1,000 and the final price is ₹4,000.

Discount Formula

The discount amount is calculated as:

Discount Amount = Original Price × Discount Percentage ÷ 100

The final sale price is:

Sale Price = Original Price − Discount Amount

Example: 20% Discount

Suppose a product has an original price of ₹5,000 and the store offers a 20% discount.

Discount = ₹5,000 × 20 ÷ 100 = ₹1,000

Therefore:

Final Price = ₹5,000 − ₹1,000 = ₹4,000

You save ₹1,000 and pay ₹4,000.

How to Find the Original Price

Sometimes you know the sale price and discount percentage but want to find the original price.

The formula is:

Original Price = Sale Price ÷ (1 − Discount Percentage ÷ 100)

For example, if an item costs ₹4,000 after a 20% discount:

Original Price = ₹4,000 ÷ 0.80 = ₹5,000

Discount vs Price Reduction

A percentage discount is calculated from the original price. Therefore, a 20% discount on ₹5,000 saves ₹1,000, while a 20% discount on ₹10,000 saves ₹2,000.

The same discount percentage can therefore result in very different savings depending on the original price.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a 20% discount?

Multiply the original price by 20 and divide by 100. Subtract the result from the original price to get the discounted price.

What is the discount on ₹5,000 at 20%?

The discount is ₹1,000 and the final price is ₹4,000.

How do I calculate the original price after a discount?

Divide the discounted price by 1 minus the discount percentage divided by 100.

Is 50% off followed by another 50% off equal to 100% off?

No. The second 50% discount is applied to the already discounted price. For example, ₹10,000 reduced by 50% becomes ₹5,000, and another 50% discount reduces it to ₹2,500. The total effective discount is 75%.

Does this calculator include GST?

No. This calculator only calculates the discount based on the prices and percentage entered. GST and other charges should be considered separately.
