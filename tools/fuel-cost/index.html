About this tool

Plan your road trip

Enter your distance, vehicle mileage and current fuel price to estimate the fuel cost of your journey.

The result is an estimate. Actual fuel consumption can vary because of traffic, driving style, road conditions, vehicle load, air conditioning and other factors.

How to Calculate Fuel Cost

Fuel cost can be estimated using the distance travelled, vehicle mileage and fuel price.

The basic formula is:

Fuel Required = Distance ÷ Vehicle Mileage

Then:

Fuel Cost = Fuel Required × Fuel Price

Example Fuel Cost Calculation

Suppose your road trip distance is 500 km, your vehicle gives a mileage of 18 km/litre and fuel costs ₹100 per litre.

Fuel required: 500 ÷ 18 = 27.78 litres

Estimated fuel cost: 27.78 × ₹100 = ₹2,777.78

One Way vs Round Trip

Select One way if the distance you entered represents only the journey from the starting point to the destination.

Select Round trip if you want the calculator to automatically double the entered distance for the return journey.

Cost Per Person

If multiple people are travelling together, enter the number of passengers. The calculator will estimate the fuel cost per passenger by dividing the total fuel cost equally among the passengers.

For example, if the total fuel cost is ₹4,000 and four people share the cost equally, the estimated fuel cost per person is ₹1,000.

What Affects Real World Fuel Consumption?

The mileage printed by a vehicle manufacturer may differ from the mileage achieved during an actual road trip.

Factors that can affect fuel consumption include traffic conditions, average speed, road quality, vehicle load, tyre pressure, driving style, air conditioning and frequent acceleration or braking.

Fuel Cost Formula

Fuel Required = Distance ÷ Mileage

Fuel Cost = Fuel Required × Fuel Price

Cost Per Kilometre = Fuel Price ÷ Mileage

Cost Per Person = Total Fuel Cost ÷ Number of Passengers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate fuel cost for a car trip?

Enter the total distance, vehicle mileage and fuel price. The calculator divides the distance by mileage to estimate fuel required and then multiplies it by the fuel price.

How is fuel required calculated?

Fuel required is calculated by dividing the journey distance in kilometres by the vehicle mileage in kilometres per litre.

Can I calculate round trip fuel cost?

Yes. Select Round trip and enter the one way distance. The calculator will automatically calculate the fuel cost for the return journey as well.

Can I calculate fuel cost per person?

Yes. Enter the number of passengers travelling in the vehicle. The calculator will divide the estimated fuel cost equally among them.

Can I use this calculator for petrol and diesel?

Yes. Enter the applicable petrol or diesel price per litre. The calculation works for either fuel type.

Does the calculator include toll charges?

No. This calculator estimates fuel expenses only. Toll charges, parking fees and other travel expenses are not included.

Is the Fuel Cost Calculator free?

Yes. India Tools Hub calculators are free to use without registration.

Are my inputs stored?

The calculation runs in your browser. Your entered values do not need to be stored to calculate the result.
