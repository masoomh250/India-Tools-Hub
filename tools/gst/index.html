How to use the GST Calculator

Enter the amount, select the applicable GST rate and choose whether you want to add GST to the amount or remove GST from an inclusive amount.

How to calculate GST?

When adding GST to a base amount, the GST amount is calculated as:

GST = Base Amount × GST Rate ÷ 100

The final amount is:

Total Amount = Base Amount + GST

How to remove GST from an inclusive amount?

When an amount already includes GST, the original base amount can be calculated using:

Base Amount = Inclusive Amount ÷ (1 + GST Rate ÷ 100)

The GST included in the amount is then:

GST = Inclusive Amount − Base Amount

GST calculation example

Suppose the base price of a product is ₹1,00,000 and the GST rate is 12%.

GST = ₹1,00,000 × 12% = ₹12,000

Total price including GST = ₹1,12,000

Example of removing GST

Suppose the final price including 12% GST is ₹80,000.

The original price before GST is approximately ₹71,428.57.

GST included in the price is approximately ₹8,571.43.

Common GST rates

This calculator currently provides commonly used GST rate options of 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%.

The applicable GST rate depends on the goods or services being supplied. Always verify the applicable rate for your specific product or service.
