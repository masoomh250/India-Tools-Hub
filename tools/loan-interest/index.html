How to Use the Loan Interest Calculator

Enter the loan principal, annual interest rate and loan tenure above. Select Calculate to instantly estimate the interest payable and total repayment amount.

Simple loan interest formula

This calculator uses the simple interest formula:

Simple Interest = Principal × Rate × Time

Where the interest rate is expressed as a decimal and the time is measured in years.

The total repayment is calculated as:

Total Repayment = Principal + Simple Interest

Example

Suppose you borrow ₹5,00,000 at an annual interest rate of 9% for 5 years.

The estimated simple interest is:

₹5,00,000 × 9% × 5 = ₹2,25,000

Therefore, the estimated total repayment would be:

₹5,00,000 + ₹2,25,000 = ₹7,25,000

Simple interest vs EMI loan calculation

This calculator uses simple interest and is useful for a quick interest estimate. Many bank and financial institution loans, such as home loans, personal loans and car loans, are normally repaid through EMIs using an amortization schedule.

If you want to calculate your monthly EMI, total interest and total amount payable for an EMI based loan, use our EMI Calculator.

Factors that affect loan interest

The total interest on a loan depends on the principal amount, interest rate and loan tenure. A higher loan amount or interest rate generally increases the interest payable. A longer tenure can also increase the total interest cost.

Why use a Loan Interest Calculator?

A loan interest calculator can help you quickly estimate borrowing costs and compare different loan amounts, interest rates and tenures before making a financial decision.

Is this a simple interest calculator?

Yes. This calculator uses the simple interest formula based on the principal amount, annual interest rate and tenure in years.

Does this calculator calculate EMI?

No. This tool calculates simple interest and total repayment. For monthly EMI calculations, use the EMI Calculator.

Does actual bank loan interest work exactly like this?

Not necessarily. Actual loan interest can depend on the lender's calculation method, repayment schedule, compounding or amortization method, fees and other loan terms. Use your lender's repayment schedule for the exact amount.

Can I use this calculator for personal loans?

You can use it for a quick simple interest estimate. However, if your personal loan is repaid through monthly EMIs, the EMI Calculator will provide a more appropriate estimate.
