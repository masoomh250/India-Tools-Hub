About this tool

Simple NPS retirement estimate

This calculator estimates how your regular NPS contributions could grow over time using an assumed annual rate of return.

Your calculations are performed in your browser. No account or registration is required.

NPS returns are market linked and actual returns can be higher or lower than the assumed rate. Annuity income also depends on the annuity option and prevailing annuity rates.

How to use the NPS Calculator

Enter your expected monthly NPS contribution, expected annual return and investment period. The calculator estimates the potential retirement corpus and shows how much you contributed versus the estimated investment growth.

How the NPS calculation works

For this calculator, monthly contributions are assumed to be invested regularly and compounded monthly using the expected annual return entered by you.

The estimated future value is calculated using the standard future value formula for regular monthly contributions:

Future Value = Monthly Contribution × (((1 + r)n − 1) ÷ r) × (1 + r)

Here, r represents the monthly rate of return and n represents the total number of monthly contributions. The calculation assumes contributions are made at the beginning of each month.

Example

Suppose you invest ₹10,000 every month for 25 years and assume an annual return of 10%. The calculator estimates the potential retirement corpus based on these assumptions. Your actual NPS corpus may differ because NPS returns are market linked.

Understanding the NPS corpus

The estimated corpus represents the total value of your NPS investment at the end of the selected investment period. It includes both your contributions and the estimated investment growth.

NPS annuity and lump sum

At a normal NPS exit, applicable rules generally allow up to 60% of the accumulated pension wealth as lump sum, while at least 40% is used for purchasing an annuity. The exact applicable rules can depend on the NPS sector, exit type and prevailing regulations.

The annuity portion is used to provide pension income. The actual pension amount cannot be calculated from the corpus alone because it depends on the annuity option and the rate offered by the annuity service provider.

Important factors affecting your NPS corpus

Your final NPS corpus can be affected by your monthly contribution, investment period, investment performance, contribution timing, applicable charges and changes in investment allocation.

NPS calculator limitations

This is an illustrative retirement planning calculator and not an official NPS statement. It does not account for every NPS rule, fund management charge, contribution variation, tax treatment, annuity pricing or individual exit condition.

What is NPS?

The National Pension System is a retirement savings system designed to help subscribers build a long term retirement corpus through contributions invested in market linked pension funds.

Is NPS return guaranteed?

No. NPS investment returns are market linked and are not guaranteed. The return entered in this calculator is only an assumption used to estimate the potential future corpus.

How much of the NPS corpus can normally be taken as lump sum?

For a normal exit, applicable NPS rules generally permit up to 60% of the accumulated pension wealth as lump sum, with at least 40% used for annuity, subject to the applicable exit rules.

Does the calculator include annuity income?

The calculator provides an indicative annuity corpus based on a 40% allocation assumption. It does not estimate the actual monthly pension because annuity rates and annuity options vary by provider and product.

Can I change my monthly NPS contribution?

Yes. In real life, your contributions can change over time. This calculator assumes the same monthly contribution throughout the selected investment period.

Are my NPS calculator inputs stored?

The calculation runs in your browser. The static calculator does not require your inputs to be sent to a server for the calculation.
