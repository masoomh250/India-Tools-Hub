How to Use the PPF Calculator

Enter your planned annual PPF contribution, interest rate and investment period. Select Calculate to estimate the total contribution, interest earned and maturity amount.

PPF Calculation Method

For this calculator, the annual contribution is assumed to be deposited at the beginning of each financial year and the entered annual interest rate is applied for the year.

The calculation is:

Year-end balance = (Previous balance + Annual contribution) × (1 + Interest Rate)

The calculation is repeated for each year of the selected tenure.

Example

Suppose you invest ₹1,50,000 every year at an assumed interest rate of 7.1% for 15 years.

Under the beginning-of-year contribution assumption used by this calculator:

Total contribution = ₹22,50,000

The estimated maturity value is approximately:

₹40.68 lakh

The estimated interest earned is approximately:

₹18.18 lakh

Important PPF Interest Calculation Rule

PPF interest is calculated for each calendar month based on the lowest balance between the close of the fifth day and the end of that month, and the interest is credited annually.

Because this calculator uses an annual contribution assumption, the actual maturity value can differ depending on when deposits are made during the financial year.

PPF Interest Rate

The PPF interest rate is notified by the Government from time to time. The default rate used in this calculator is 7.1% per annum.

You can change the interest rate in the calculator if the applicable rate changes.

PPF Contribution Limit

The applicable maximum PPF subscription is ₹1,50,000 in a financial year. Contributions can generally be made as a lump sum or through instalments, subject to the applicable scheme rules.

PPF Tenure

The standard PPF maturity period is 15 years. After the initial maturity period, the account can be extended in blocks of five years, subject to the applicable rules.

Why Use a PPF Calculator?

A PPF calculator helps estimate how regular long-term contributions can grow through compounding. It can be useful for planning retirement savings and other long-term financial goals.

What is the PPF interest rate used by this calculator?

The default rate used by this calculator is 7.1% per annum. PPF rates can change based on Government notifications, so you can update the rate in the calculator when required.

What is the maximum PPF contribution per year?

The applicable maximum subscription is ₹1,50,000 in a financial year. Contributions may be made in a lump sum or instalments subject to the applicable rules.

Is PPF interest calculated monthly?

Yes. PPF interest is calculated for each calendar month based on the lowest balance between the close of the fifth day and the end of the month, and the interest is credited annually.

Does this calculator give the exact PPF maturity amount?

No. This is an estimate based on an annual contribution made at the beginning of each year. Actual PPF maturity can differ depending on deposit dates, applicable interest rates and the actual account balance.

Can I calculate PPF for more than 15 years?

Yes. This calculator allows longer periods in five-year increments to represent PPF extensions after the initial 15-year maturity period.

Is PPF a tax-saving investment?

PPF has tax benefits under applicable tax rules. Tax treatment can depend on the applicable law and individual circumstances, so consult the latest official tax guidance before making investment decisions.

Are my PPF inputs stored?

The calculation runs in your browser. This static website does not require your PPF inputs to calculate the result.
