Salary Calculator: How It Works

A salary calculator helps estimate the amount you may receive after selected employee deductions from your annual CTC. The calculation is useful for comparing job offers and understanding the approximate monthly salary from a given CTC.

Salary calculation formula

For this calculator, the estimated annual salary is calculated as:

Estimated Annual Salary = Annual CTC − Employee PF − Other Annual Deductions

The estimated monthly salary is then:

Estimated Monthly Salary = Estimated Annual Salary ÷ 12

Example

Suppose your annual CTC is ₹12,00,000 and your employee PF contribution is ₹21,600 per year, with no other deductions entered.

The estimated annual salary would be:

₹12,00,000 − ₹21,600 = ₹11,78,400

The estimated monthly salary would therefore be approximately:

₹98,200 per month

CTC vs take-home salary

CTC, or Cost to Company, is not necessarily the same as the amount credited to your bank account every month. CTC can include components such as employer PF contributions, gratuity, bonuses, insurance and other benefits.

Your actual monthly credit can also depend on income tax or TDS, professional tax, employee PF, insurance, salary structure and other payroll deductions.

Therefore, this calculator provides an estimate based only on the deductions you enter. For an income tax estimate, use the Income Tax Calculator.

What can affect your actual salary?

The actual salary credited to your bank account may differ from this estimate because of several factors, including:

Employee PF, professional tax, income tax or TDS, insurance deductions, bonuses, variable pay, gratuity, employer contributions and the structure of your compensation package.

Why use a Salary Calculator?

A salary calculator can help you quickly compare different CTC offers, estimate your monthly income and understand the effect of employee deductions on your expected salary.

Is CTC the same as take-home salary?

No. CTC represents the overall cost to the company and may include employer contributions, benefits, bonuses and other components. Take-home salary is the amount credited after applicable employee deductions.

Does this calculator include income tax?

No. Income tax or TDS is not automatically calculated by this tool. If you want to include tax as a deduction, you can enter it under Other annual deductions. For a dedicated tax calculation, use the Income Tax Calculator.

Does this calculator include employer PF?

No. Employer PF is generally a component of CTC rather than an employee deduction from the salary credited to your bank account. The calculator only subtracts the Employee PF amount entered by you.

Can I use this calculator to compare job offers?

Yes. You can enter the CTC and applicable deductions for different offers to get a quick estimate of the annual and monthly salary after the deductions entered.

Are my salary inputs stored?

The calculation runs in your browser. This static website does not require your salary inputs to calculate the result.
