About this tool

Plan your complete trip budget

Add your expected travel, hotel, food, sightseeing, toll, parking and other expenses to estimate the total trip cost.

This is a planning estimate. Actual expenses may vary depending on prices, bookings, route, season, travel style and unexpected expenses.

How to Use the Trip Cost Calculator

Enter the estimated amount for each expense category. Then enter the number of people travelling together and select Calculate trip cost.

The calculator adds all the expense categories and calculates both the total trip cost and estimated cost per person.

What Expenses Should You Include?

A realistic travel budget should include more than just transportation and hotel costs.

Travel / Transport: Include flights, trains, buses, cabs or vehicle rental expenses.

Accommodation: Include hotels, resorts, homestays, camps or other lodging.

Food & Meals: Include breakfast, lunch, dinner, snacks and drinks.

Activities & Sightseeing: Include attraction tickets, safaris, tours and other activities.

Tolls: Useful for road trips where highway tolls are part of the journey.

Parking: Include parking charges at hotels, attractions and other locations.

Other Expenses: Use this category for shopping, local transport, tips, miscellaneous expenses or an emergency budget.

Trip Cost Formula

The total estimated trip cost is calculated as:

Total Trip Cost = Travel + Stay + Food + Activities + Tolls + Parking + Other Expenses

Estimated cost per person is:

Cost Per Person = Total Trip Cost ÷ Number of Travellers

Example

Suppose four people are travelling together and their estimated expenses are:

Travel: ₹12,000

Stay: ₹10,000

Food: ₹8,000

Activities: ₹5,000

Tolls: ₹2,000

Parking: ₹500

Other expenses: ₹1,000

The estimated total trip cost is ₹38,500.

For four travellers, the estimated cost per person is ₹9,625.

How to Build a Better Travel Budget

Start by estimating the major fixed expenses such as transportation and accommodation. Then add variable expenses such as food and activities.

For road trips, remember to account for fuel, tolls and parking. Keeping a small amount for unexpected expenses can also make the budget more realistic.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does this Trip Cost Calculator include?

It calculates travel, accommodation, food, activities, tolls, parking and other expenses and provides the total trip cost and estimated cost per person.

Can I use this calculator for a family trip?

Yes. Enter the combined expenses for the trip and enter the total number of travellers to estimate the cost per person.

Can I use this for a road trip?

Yes. Add fuel costs under Travel or Transport, and enter toll and parking expenses separately.

Can I calculate the cost for two people?

Yes. Enter 2 as the number of travellers. The total trip cost will be divided equally between the two travellers.

Does the calculator include taxes and service charges?

Only if you include them in the relevant expense amount. The calculator does not automatically add hotel taxes, service charges, booking fees or other charges.

Does it include fuel cost?

Yes. You can include your estimated fuel expense under Travel / Transport. For a detailed fuel calculation, use the Fuel Cost Calculator on India Tools Hub.

Is this Trip Cost Calculator free?

Yes. India Tools Hub calculators are free to use without registration.

Are my travel expenses stored?

The calculation runs in your browser. The calculator does not need to store the amounts you enter to calculate the result.
