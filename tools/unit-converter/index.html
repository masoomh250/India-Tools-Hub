What is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter helps you convert a measurement from one unit to another while preserving the same physical quantity.

For example, you can convert kilometres to miles, kilograms to pounds, litres to gallons, square metres to square feet or kilometres per hour to miles per hour.

Supported Unit Conversions

This calculator supports several commonly used conversion categories.

Convert between kilometres, metres, centimetres, millimetres, miles, yards, feet and inches.

Weight conversions

Convert between kilograms, grams, pounds and ounces.

Temperature conversions

Area conversions

Convert between square metres, square kilometres, square feet, square yards, acres and hectares.

Volume conversions

Convert between litres, millilitres, US gallons, US fluid ounces and cubic metres.

Speed conversions

Convert between kilometres per hour, miles per hour and metres per second.

Temperature Conversion

Temperature conversion is different from most unit conversions because the scales have different starting points.

For example:

Celsius to Fahrenheit = (C × 9 ÷ 5) + 32

Fahrenheit to Celsius = (F − 32) × 5 ÷ 9

Kelvin is an absolute temperature scale:

Celsius to Kelvin = Celsius + 273.15

Accuracy and Rounding

The calculator performs the conversion using standard conversion factors and displays the result to four decimal places where appropriate.

For very large or very small values, the displayed result may be rounded. The underlying conversion is performed using JavaScript number arithmetic in your browser.

Can I convert kilometres to miles?

Yes. Select Length, choose Kilometres as the source unit and Miles as the target unit.

Can I convert kilograms to pounds?
